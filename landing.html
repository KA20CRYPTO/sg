The 3-Step AI Hiring Workflow

A clean, explainable AI hiring loop that keeps HR in control.

1. Data Ingestion

Resumes and profiles are normalized, structured, and matched to job criteria.

2. AI Analysis

Multi-dimensional scoring, bias checks, and success predictions are generated.

3. Human Review

HR validates the transparent AI output and makes the final hiring decision.

The 3-Step Candidate AI Career Workflow

A transparent, AI-powered journey that helps candidates understand, improve, and grow.

1. Resume Upload

Candidates upload their resume to get it structured, parsed, and prepared for AI evaluation.

2. AI Skill Analysis

AI scores the resume, identifies missing skills, role fit, ATS readiness, and optimization gaps.

3. Career Optimization

Candidates receive actionable suggestions to improve resumes, skills, projects, and job matching.
